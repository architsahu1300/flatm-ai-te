Standard Clauses

1.

Term of Tenancy

The tenancy shall be for a period of 11 months, commencing on 01 October 2023 and ending on 31 August 2024.

2.

Rent & Deposit

The monthly rent is agreed at ₹45,000, payable on or before the 5th of every month. A refundable security deposit of ₹1,500,000 has been paid.

3.

Maintenance Charges

Society maintenance charges are included in the monthly rent. Any extraordinary levies by the society will be borne by the Owner.
