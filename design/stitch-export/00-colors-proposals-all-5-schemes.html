# Homley Color Scheme Proposals This document outlines five distinct color identities for Homley. Each is designed to be subtle, memorable, and high-contrast (AA-checked). Neutrals handle 90% of the UI, while the accent color is reserved for primary actions, active states, and AI moments. --- ## 1. Terra & Cream (Warm Earth) **Rationale:** A grounded, organic palette that replaces the coldness of tech with the warmth of a home. Terracotta provides a "human" and "approachable" touch, while cream and warm stone neutrals create a premium, gallery-like feel. It avoids "SaaS blue" entirely, feeling more like a high-end lifestyle brand (Airbnb/Notion) than a utility. ### Light Theme - **Page Background:** `#FCF9F7` (Cream) - **Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) - **Secondary Surface:** `#F5F1EE` (Warm Stone) - **Hairline Border:** `#E8E2DD` - **Primary Text:** `#2D2926` (Warm Charcoal) - **Muted Text:** `#736C66` - **Brand/Accent:** `#BC5E40` (Terracotta) - **Brand-Hover:** `#A34F35` - **Brand-Soft:** `#F8EBE6` - **Success/Soft:** `#4A7256` / `#E9F0EB` - **Warning/Soft:** `#D18E33` / `#FBF2E6` - **Danger/Soft:** `#B44D4D` / `#F7EBEB` ### Dark Theme - **Page Background:** `#1A1817` - **Card Surface:** `#242120` - **Secondary Surface:** `#2D2928` - **Hairline Border:** `#3D3836` - **Primary Text:** `#F2EFED` - **Muted Text:** `#A39D98` - **Brand/Accent:** `#D98066` (Softened Terracotta) - **Brand-Hover:** `#E09580` - **Brand-Soft:** `#3D2D28` - **Success/Soft:** `#76A685` / `#1D2620` - **Warning/Soft:** `#E6B36A` / `#2B231A` - **Danger/Soft:** `#D67C7C` / `#2B1D1D` ### Match Ring Mapping - **Score ≥80:** `#4A7256` (Success Green) - **60–79:** `#D18E33` (Warning Amber) - **<60:** `#736C66` (Muted Neutral) --- ## 2.
